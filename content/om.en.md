## Welcome to a world of botany

Spira is more than a name. It refers to how a seed bursts through the earth, how a branch shoots up in spring, how a movement takes root from below. Botany is about life — and that life we want to celebrate.

This is a place created from a genuine passion for plants and a desire to share that joy with everyone who wants more green in their lives.

## Our story

My journey in the plant world started with a single monstera on a windowsill in a small student flat. It was love at first sight — a beauty that didn't need a south-facing window, was happy with weekly watering and yet grew upward day by day.

Soon more plants followed. Peace lilies that cleaned the air and thrived in the shady corner by the sofa. Aloe plants that took care of themselves during summer vacation. Orchids that demanded patience but rewarded with explosions of flowers every winter.

Over the years I have learned that every plant has its own rhythm, its own personality. Listening to them — reading when they're thirsty, when they need more light, when they're thriving — that is the true art of botany.

## What Spira offers

Here you'll find:

- **Plant profiles** with detailed care instructions, origin and characteristics
- **Beginner guides** to help you get started step by step
- **Tips on light, watering and soil** for every type of plant
- **Inspiration** for a greener and more vibrant home

All content is written with care, based on real experience and botanical knowledge.

## Botany for everyone

Spira is written for everyone — from the person who has just bought their very first cactus to the one who already has a full-scale botanical home with hundreds of plants.

Botany is not reserved for experts. It is an art that anyone can learn, one leaf at a time. It just requires curiosity, a little patience and a willingness to listen to what grows.

> _"To grow a plant is to enter into a contract with life itself."_

We're glad you're here. Now, let's grow together.
