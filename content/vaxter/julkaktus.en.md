The Christmas cactus is the gentlest of cacti: no thorns, simple needs, and a December bloom that blankets the plant. Specimens live for generations and are passed down as heirlooms.

## Origin and natural habitat

Schlumbergera grows as an epiphyte on tree branches in Brazil's Atlantic forest, in shade and humidity — a world apart from desert cacti. Hence its preference for indirect light and moderate moisture.

## Care

### Light

Bright indirect spot. It enjoys a shady balcony in summer. Avoid moving it while buds form — it dislikes change.

### Watering

Let the top of the soil dry slightly between waterings. More moisture than a desert cactus, less than a typical houseplant. Mist budding plants occasionally.

### Soil and feeding

Airy potting soil with perlite. For December buds: a cool spot (15–18 °C) and twelve hours of darkness per day from October. Feed during the growing season.

## Common problems

**Bud drop** comes from moving, draughts, or big temperature swings. **Rot at the joints** means overwatering. **White cottony tufts** are mealybugs — treat with alcohol or soapy water.

## Good to know

Well cared for, a Christmas cactus can live for over fifty years and grow into a truly large hanging plant. Safe for cats and dogs.
