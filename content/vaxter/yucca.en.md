Yucca looks like a mini palm with editorial style: thick woody trunk, sword-green leaves and almost no demands at all. Give it sun and it thrives for years.

## Origin and natural habitat

Yucca elephantipes comes from dry parts of Mexico and Central America, where it grows as a small tree. There it endures both drought and fierce sun — two traits that made it a classic houseplant.

## Care

### Light

A south-facing window is yucca paradise — the more direct light the better. In dim spots the leaves grow thin and droopy.

### Watering

Water thoroughly but rarely: let the soil dry out completely between waterings, usually every two to three weeks. In winter once a month is often enough.

### Soil and feeding

Sandy, free-draining soil — cactus mix works well. Feed sparingly. Repot only when the pot cracks; the root system is small.

## Common problems

**Brown leaf tips** usually come from dry air or sparse watering — trim with scissors. **Soft trunks** mean root rot from overwatering. **Yellow lower leaves** are natural ageing.

## Good to know

In its native range yuccas are pollinated exclusively by a single small moth — one of nature's most specialised symbioses. Note: toxic to pets.
